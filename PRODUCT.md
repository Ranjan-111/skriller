# Skriller

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, React and TypeScript frontend, Python API and background workers, PostgreSQL. Stack follows docs/TECH_STACK.md; implementation authorised by the user.

## Users

Initial hypothesis from the PRD: small B2B founders, agencies and sales/research teams qualifying prospects and investigating companies. Customer niche remains unconfirmed.

## Product Purpose

A prompt-first lead-generation and research assistant. Describe a research task, discover candidates, review evidence and refine or export results.

## Operating Context

Users work with conversational research requests, professional profiles, company websites and discussion sources. LinkedIn is the requested primary professional source; Reddit is a launch research priority.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Frontend scaffold must distinguish unavailable services from empty results. No invented prospects, counts, evidence or live-source capabilities. Source availability is determined by configured connectors. Initial UI is a scaffold; job execution arrives in later tickets.

## Brand Commitments

Skriller is the working name; branding undecided. Prompt is the primary interaction; optional compact controls supplement it.

## Evidence on Hand

Authoritative PRD, tech stack, ticket backlog and source snapshots exist under docs/ and reference/. No customer testimonials, live research results or benchmark claims exist.

## Product Principles

- Evidence accompanies factual results.
- Missing information stays explicit.
- User intent starts with a prompt.
- Genuine execution states replace fabricated progress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable web interface with visible focus, meaningful labels and readable contrast. Responsive desktop and mobile web.
